# Programs, companies and reading: Iman Gadzhi

What he has built and sold, and what he reads, as he describes it in public. Nothing here copies paid course material.

## Programs he teaches
- **Six Figure SMMA, then Agency Incubator, then Agency Navigator (2017 to 2022).** His agency course. Week two is all mindset (focus, sleep, social circle, self-image, visualization, momentum), which he calls the most transformative part. Taught with "plug and play" worksheets and daily action steps.
- **Copy Paste Agency and Meridian Mastermind.** Higher tiers for agency owners already signing clients.
- **Grow Your Agency / growyouragency.com.** The early education brand; he calls it his passion project tied to reforming education.
- **Digital Launchpad and Monetize (2024 to 2025).** Low-ticket self-education platform and a micro personal brand program.
- **Consulting.com (bought 2025) and Uplevel.** Business education with AI tools; he presents clients as clients, not students.

## Companies and stakes he talks about
- **IAG Media:** his ad agency (2016 to 2022), info-product and e-commerce clients, one employee plus contractors at its leanest.
- **Whop:** co-owner since 2025; thesis that digital products for creators are the big market.
- **Higgsfield AI (investor and advisor) and ListKit (stake):** AI content and outbound tooling, wired into Consulting.com.
- **HILLS eyewear and Gadzhi clothing:** passion brands he says he does not run for profit.

## How he teaches in public
- Long YouTube presentations with slides, three to seventeen numbered points, his own numbers and friends' cases, a small challenge at the end, and a program link.
- Separate channels by audience: main channel (beginners), business channel (owners above $250k a year), vlog channel (lifestyle and his product brand).
- Weekly story-based emails, written as a Sunday ritual.

## Books he credits
- **Psycho-Cybernetics (Maxwell Maltz):** the basis of his self-image belief. "I would say this is pretty much the nucleus to most modern self-help books" (S012)
- **Reality Transurfing:** named with Psycho-Cybernetics as a favourite.
- **The Shallows:** why he distrusts social media.
- **Ogilvy on Advertising and Claude Hopkins:** his copywriting recommendations.
- **Felix Dennis, an Onassis biography, Meditations (Marcus Aurelius):** on wealth and life (S012).
- **The Alchemist, The 4-Hour Workweek, Influence, Never Split the Difference, How to Win Friends and Influence People, a book on the Federal Reserve:** his "17 books" list (S030).
- **The Everything Store and Good to Great:** the flywheel and customer obsession (S047, S052).
- **Course resource decks** on Elon Musk, Profit First, The Outsiders and What Doesn't Kill Us: summaries he hands out, not his own words.
