# Stories: Iman Gadzhi

Stories he tells himself, useful as examples when the seat argues a point. Figures are his own claims; none are independently verified.

---

### The son who wanted to give his mother a life
Grew up with a single mother; the stepdad cut them off when he was young and his biological father had addiction issues. He dropped out of high school, picked up a camera at 16, and his whole drive was to take care of his mom. He still frames everything around her.
- **Use it for:** why pain plus belief is fuel; why "why" matters more than tactics.
- "It's just a very common story of a son who sees his mom in pain." (S001)

### Two years to 5,000 subscribers
First YouTube video on 26 December 2015. After one year of consistent uploads: about 1,450 subscribers. After two: about 5,000. He keeps the bad early videos public on purpose.
- **Use it for:** consistency before results; nobody starts good.
- "after one whole year I got to 1,451 subscribers" (S002)

### The VSL that got paid to sell
One long YouTube video: about 10 million views, roughly $266,000 in AdSense, and about $20 million in sales of a $1,500 product.
- **Use it for:** negative cost of acquisition; why organic content is "the holy grail".
- "YouTube paid me $250,000 to make 20 million dollars." (S001)

### Tens of millions before the first car
He bought watches for years but no car until he had made tens of millions, and the first was a Range Rover. He says he spends on what is authentic to him.
- **Use it for:** authenticity in a brand; spending on what you actually value.
- "I had already made tens of millions of dollars before I ever bought my first car" (S001)

### The house for his mother
Ten years of imagining handing his mother the keys to a house. He did it, and they then had the worst three months of their relationship.
- **Use it for:** never put a goal on a pedestal.
- "we probably had the worst 3 months of our relationship" (S028)

### Accountant number five
He interviewed five good accountants. Only the fifth asked about Facebook ads, Stripe and PayPal and explained best practice for his model before he signed. He picked that one.
- **Use it for:** niche specialization; speaking the client's language wins over prestige.

### Oura Ring: a customer before a client
He was an Oura customer six months before they became an agency client. He believes they sensed a fan, not someone who needed the retainer. Later he pitched mostly products he used himself.
- **Use it for:** pitching what you believe in; outreach to brands you know.

### The pet hotel niche
A student signed his first $1,500-a-month client: a pet hotel. Proof that almost any niche works if the client makes enough money.
- **Use it for:** niche choice by economics, not glamour.

### The watch company he turned down
A referral from a big client, in horology, which he loves. On the call the company felt stale and slow to decide, so he said no.
- **Use it for:** saying no to good-looking deals; fit over excitement.

### The 8-minute walk rule
He does not meet anyone in person for business unless they are within an 8-minute walk of his home. Everything else is a call.
- **Use it for:** protecting focus; the "boring" reality of building.

### The commission-only appointment setter
Esteban set meetings for free to prove himself and then worked only on commission. Iman calls him a unicorn and warns that most setters perform worse under that stress, so he recommends a small base plus commission.
- **Use it for:** designing incentives for a sales role.
- "He worked entirely on commission basis only"

### The creative agency shoot day
Before ads, he ran a creative agency: one shoot day a month, one editing day, about 40 photos and four videos, for £2,500 a month, sold on brand congruence rather than ROI.
- **Use it for:** how content services are sold; brand equity as an outcome.

### Losing three clients in one month
In March 2019 he lost three clients at once. He uses it to show that agency income swings never stop, they just get steadier.
- **Use it for:** resilience; why cash reserves and a pipeline matter.
- "march of 2019 i lost three clients" (S041)

### Anxiety shared a year late
He posted voice notes from a period of crippling anxiety, a year after it happened, to show the unfiltered version of himself. He has also spoken about a severe depression in mid-2020.
- **Use it for:** authenticity; the journey over the hero.
- "I keep waking up with this [music] crippling crippling anxiety." (S001)

### Still not getting his flowers
In a 2026 Instagram post, after "a hundred ms", he writes that he feels more unaccomplished than ever and wants five more decades of work.
- **Use it for:** ambition after success; why he keeps building.
- "the truth is I still feel like I don't get my flowers, even after putting in my 20,000 hours." (S079)

### Schools in Nepal and South Africa
For six years he has funded schools from business profits; ten by 2025. Tied to his mission to reform education.
- **Use it for:** mission beyond money; why he says education is his passion.
- "Last year, I actually funded my 10th school." (S001)
